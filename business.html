FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

Is ScamRatio a replacement for a security team?

No. It is designed as an awareness and checking layer that can complement your organization's existing security, IT and incident-response processes.

Can employees use it without technical training?

The business experience is intentionally designed around simple actions: check, understand the warning signals, report when appropriate and learn from the pattern.

Does this page currently connect to a business backend?

The page is fully interactive on the frontend. The demo request form currently provides local confirmation. A real CRM, email or database connection can be added once your backend endpoint is available.

Can ScamRatio support awareness campaigns?

The Scam IQ challenge and educational workflows can be used as awareness content. A production campaign system would require a backend for accounts, reporting and organization-level analytics.
